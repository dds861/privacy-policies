Privacy Policy — Time Difference Calculator

App package: com.dd.timeleftcounter
Last updated: November 1, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Android application Time Difference Calculator (com.dd.timeleftcounter). Developer and privacy contact: Daniyar Nurgaliyev. Email: adeveloper861@gmail.com.

Information used for calculations

The app uses the dates and times you enter to calculate the difference between time periods. Using the core calculation features does not require an account or submission of your name, email address or phone number. Depending on the app version, some preferences or recently entered values may be saved locally on your device.

Google AdMob advertising and third-party data

Some versions of the app have included Google AdMob advertising, provided by Google LLC. Where the advertising SDK is included and active, Google may automatically collect and share the following information: your IP address (which may indicate an approximate location), app usage and ad interactions, performance and crash diagnostics, and device or advertising identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID and App Set ID). This information is used to deliver and measure advertising, perform analytics, and prevent fraud. Google processes this information according to its own privacy terms. Data sent by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit.

Storage, retention and deletion

Preferences and values stored locally by the app can be deleted by clearing the app's data in Android settings or uninstalling the app. Data processed by the advertising provider is subject to Google's retention and deletion policies. Contact us at the email above for privacy inquiries, including requests concerning data access or deletion.

Your choices and rights

You can manage the advertising identifier through your device settings. You may contact us to request access, correction or deletion of applicable data. We may revise this policy when app functionality or legal requirements change; the date at the top indicates the latest revision.
